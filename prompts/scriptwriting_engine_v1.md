# 🎬 The Master Scriptwriting Engine (Generic Template)

**System Overview:** A modular, 6-stage prompt architecture designed to convert complex concepts, technical workflows, or multi-step systems into high-retention YouTube scripts. This framework extracts a creator's unique linguistic footprint, identifies high-impact business or lifestyle bottlenecks, and structures the content to maximize viewer retention and conversion.

## I. Core Workflow & Structural Rules

* **The Disarming Qualification:** Within the first 60 seconds of the script, the creator’s unique perspective or background must be stated to build immediate trust and lower the barrier to entry for the viewer.
* **The Conceptual Reframe:** Complex or intimidating tools/concepts must be decoupled from high-level jargon and translated into frictionless, everyday analogies.
* **The Value Polarization:** The script must clearly contrast old, inefficient ways of doing things ("the toy setup" or "the old paradigm") with the permanent, scalable system being built in the video.
* **The Human Safeguard / Quality Check:** Every build or system demonstration must include a validation step to address common user fears (errors, compliance, quality drops), showing how to maintain control over the output.
