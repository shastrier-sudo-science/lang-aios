# Proposal Polish Prompt
## Tighten the Draft Before You Hit Send

**Use this prompt in Claude or ChatGPT AFTER running the Structure Prompt.**

---

```
You are a ruthless editor. I have written a client proposal that needs to be tightened before sending.

MY PROPOSAL DRAFT:
[Paste your proposal here]

Apply these edits in order:

1. REMOVE: Every sentence that talks about YOU ("I have 5 years of experience", "I am passionate about", "I believe"). Replace with sentences that talk about THE CLIENT's outcome.
2. REMOVE: Every hedge word ("just", "maybe", "perhaps", "I think", "hopefully"). Replace with certainty.
3. REMOVE: Every paragraph longer than 3 sentences. Break into bullets or shorter paragraphs.
4. ADD: One specific number or timeframe to the mechanism section. (e.g., "within 48 hours" not "soon")
5. ADD: One risk reversal sentence to the scope section. (e.g., "If the first draft doesn't meet the success metric, I'll revise at no cost.")
6. CHECK: Does the price appear only once? If it appears more than once, remove the extras. The price should be a single confident number, not a negotiation.
7. CHECK: Does the next step require a yes/no response? If not, rewrite it so it does.

Return the polished proposal. Then list the 3 most important changes you made and why.
```

---

## Why This Works

The difference between a $100 proposal and a $1,000 proposal is usually 3 sentences. This prompt removes the 3 sentences that kill deals and adds the 3 sentences that close them.

**The 3 killers:**
- Talking about yourself ("I have experience") instead of the client's outcome
- Hedge words that signal uncertainty ("maybe", "I think")
- Long paragraphs that the client will not read

**The 3 closers:**
- Specific numbers that create confidence ("within 48 hours")
- Risk reversal that removes the client's fear ("revise at no cost")
- A single price that signals certainty (not a range that signals negotiation)

---

## What to Do With the Output

Apply the 3 most important changes manually. Do not blindly accept the AI's edits. The AI is your editor, not your ghostwriter. Your voice must remain in the final proposal. Then send it. Move on. The next proposal is waiting.
