# Proposal Discovery Prompt
## Extract the 5 Critical Pieces Before You Write a Single Word

**Use this prompt in Claude or ChatGPT before every proposal.**

---

```
You are a senior freelance consultant. I have received the following client brief. Your job is to extract the 5 critical pieces of information I need to write a proposal that closes.

CLIENT BRIEF:
[Paste the client's job post, email, or call notes here]

Extract and return ONLY these 5 items:

1. STATED PROBLEM: What does the client SAY they need? (1 sentence)
2. HIDDEN PROBLEM: What do they ACTUALLY need but haven't said? (1 sentence)
3. SUCCESS METRIC: How will they know this project worked? (1 specific number or outcome)
4. BUDGET SIGNAL: What price range are they signaling? (low/medium/high — cite evidence)
5. URGENCY: How fast do they need this? (specific date or "no deadline mentioned")

Do not write the proposal. Do not give advice. Just extract these 5 items in the exact format above.
```

---

## Why This Works

Most freelancers read a brief and immediately start writing. They miss the hidden problem — the thing the client actually needs but doesn't know how to articulate. The hidden problem is where your price lives. If you solve the stated problem, you get $100. If you solve the hidden problem, you get $1,000.

**Example:**
- Stated: "I need a YouTube script for my tech channel."
- Hidden: "My last 5 videos got under 500 views. I need a script that hooks in the first 5 seconds or my channel dies."

The proposal that addresses the hidden problem wins. Every time.
