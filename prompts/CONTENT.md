## VOICE DNA (non-negotiable, every issue)

Punchy, short-to-medium sentences. Paragraphs never exceed 3 sentences. Bold key terms for scannability. Grounded, tactile vocabulary (messy, clean, boxes, sludge, bleeding, trapped, clock ticking). Speak as a patient, encouraging friend to absolute AI beginners. Open mid-scene with a raw personal constraint. First person only, never third.

State hard personal facts plainly, in the same tone as everything else: no lingering, no sympathy bid. Move from memoir into direct instruction using "you" without a hard transition. Anchor every claim in a concrete autobiographical number from real life. Commit fully to one extended analogy per issue rather than switching between several.

**Banned forever:** leverage, journey, game-changer, unlock, seamlessly, synergy, circle back, touch base, delve, robust, empower. No emoji.

**Relaxed ban (permitted, part of authentic voice):** utilize, transform/transforming.

**Optional flagship move:** Stack rhetorical questions in runs of 2-3 before answering, but keep sentences short.

## STRUCTURAL RULES

1. **SLPC every issue:** Story → Lesson → Pivot → Commandment/CTA.
2. **One tiered system, not three tips:** Starter → Decision-Grade → Publication-Ready. One Validation Check.
3. **Name the failure, not the fix:** one proprietary concept name per issue.
4. **Beginner-friendly by default:** simple definition → everyday analogy → why it matters → simple example.
5. **Swarm is not optional:** repurposed assets generated in the same pass as the draft, only after a READY verdict.
