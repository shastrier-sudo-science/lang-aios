## How This Course Works

**The Rule:** Every question you answer must produce a *tangible asset*—a script, a spreadsheet, a prompt, a client deliverable, or an updated financial model. No passive reading. If you can't build, calculate, or script it, you haven't learned it.

**The Build:** Each day's question builds on the previous day's answer. By Day 30, you'll have a complete freelance business infrastructure *and* a personal financial model that maps your path to $3.35M net worth.

**The Log:** After each session, log your answer in `logs/YYYY-MM-DD-synthesis.md` using the format from your `daily-question-generator.md`.
